
# λφ LambdaPhi Foundation

**Making software, challenging the status quo.**

The LambdaPhi Foundation is an umbrella for projects that lean toward formal logic, low-level systems, and a bit of "cursed" engineering. We aren't trying to run a corporation; we're just providing a space to experiment with how computers actually work.

### --> Software & Learning
We build software, but not to provide "convenient solutions." Modern tools are often black boxes that hide the reality of the machine behind layers of automation. 

Our goal is to create tools that encourage people to step away from that dependency. We believe in a "learning-by-doing" approach—where the value isn't in the most efficient way to solve a problem, but in the way that forces you to understand the system by heart. We'd rather you build it the "hard way" once and actually understand it than use a framework you can't explain.

### --> The Iconoclastic Approach
At our core, we are iconoclastic. We like to question the "industry standard" and tear down the assumption that there is only one "correct" way to build a system. 

Whether it's ignoring traditional workflows, embracing "cursed" engineering, or iterating through a hundred versions of a project just to see where it breaks, we do things that challenge the norm. We aren't looking for the most polished product—we're looking for the most honest understanding of the machine.

**Gluttony for the machine, pushing software to the extreme.**
